RECIPE ID: 1
RECIPE NAME: Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani
REGION / CUISINE: Telangana / Hyderabad
CATEGORY: Main Course

PREPARATION STEPS:
1. Wash and soak basmati rice for about 20 minutes.
2. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, turmeric, salt, lemon juice, and biryani spices.
3. Rest the marinated chicken for at least 30 minutes.
4. Fry sliced onions until golden and keep some aside.
5. Cook rice with whole spices until about 70 percent done.
6. Place marinated chicken in a heavy-bottomed pot and add the partially cooked rice.
7. Add fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
8. Cover tightly and cook on low heat until the chicken and rice are fully cooked.
9. Rest the biryani for a few minutes.
10. Gently mix and serve hot.

RECIPE ID: 2
RECIPE NAME: Lucknowi Chicken Biryani
REGION / CUISINE: Uttar Pradesh
CATEGORY: Main Course

PREPARATION STEPS:
1. Wash and soak basmati rice.
2. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, salt, and mild spices.
3. Fry onions until lightly golden.
4. Cook rice with cardamom, cinnamon, cloves, and bay leaf until partially cooked.
5. Cook the marinated chicken separately until nearly tender.
6. Layer chicken and rice in a heavy pot.
7. Add fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk.
8. Cover and cook slowly until done.
9. Rest before opening the lid.
10. Serve carefully without breaking the rice grains.

CATEGORY: Appetizer

PREPARATION STEPS:

RecipeFinder/
├── data/
│   └── recipes.txt
└── src/
    ├── Main.java
    ├── Recipe.java
    ├── RecipeReader.java
    └── StringAlgorithms.java

Important:
This is plain text rather than CSV. The Java reader can use separators such as
RECIPE ID:, RECIPE NAME:, REGION / CUISINE:, CATEGORY:, and PREPARATION STEPS:
to identify each record and its ordered steps.

CO2 searches can operate on recipe names and the complete recipe text, including steps.
