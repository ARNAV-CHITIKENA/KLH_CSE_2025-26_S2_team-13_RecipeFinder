RECIPE ID: 101
RECIPE NAME: Bibimbap
REGION / CUISINE: South Korea
CATEGORY: Main Course

PREPARATION STEPS:
1. Prepare the ingredients for Bibimbap, including vegetables, protein, rice, or noodles.
2. Mix soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, chilli paste, or other Korean seasonings.
3. Heat a skillet, wok, pot, or grill.
4. Cook the main ingredients until browned or tender.
5. Add the seasoning mixture and toss thoroughly.
6. Add rice, noodles, broth, or vegetables as required.
7. Cook until the ingredients are fully done and the flavors combine.
8. Add sesame seeds, spring onion, or fresh herbs.
9. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
10. Serve hot.
