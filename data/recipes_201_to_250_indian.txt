RECIPE ID: 201
RECIPE NAME: Sabudana Khichdi
REGION / CUISINE: Maharashtra
CATEGORY: Breakfast
BUDGET: 400 INR (approx.)
DURATION: 40 minutes

PREPARATION STEPS:
1. Prepare the ingredients for Sabudana Khichdi, including the main component, aromatics, and seasonings.
2. Wash, cut, marinate, or otherwise prepare the main ingredients.
3. Prepare the sauce, batter, marinade, or seasoning mixture.
4. Heat a suitable pan, pot, grill, oven, or cooking surface.
5. Cook the aromatics and spices until fragrant.
6. Add the main ingredients and cook until they begin to brown or soften.
7. Add the prepared sauce or seasoning and cook thoroughly.
8. Add water, stock, or another liquid when required and cook until tender.
9. Adjust seasoning and consistency, then add the traditional garnish.
10. Serve hot or fresh with a suitable accompaniment.
