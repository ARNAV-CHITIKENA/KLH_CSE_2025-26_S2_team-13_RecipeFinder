CATEGORY: Main Course
BUDGET: 1800 INR (approx.)
DURATION: 120 minutes

PREPARATION STEPS:
1. Wash and soak basmati rice for about 20 minutes.

3. Rest the seasoned ingredients so the flavors develop.
4. Fry sliced onions until golden and prepare the remaining garnish.
